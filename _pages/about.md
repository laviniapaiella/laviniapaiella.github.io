Welcome! I am a 4th-year PhD student in the Astrophysics and Cosmology group at the [Gran Sasso Science Institute](https://www.gssi.it/), working with Prof. Manuel Arca Sedda and Prof. Gor Oganesyan. My research investigates the formation and dynamical assembly of **intermediate-mass black holes (IMBHs)** in star clusters and their characterization through **gravitational waves**.

IMBHs have been a long-standing puzzle in astrophysics since the first hints of their existence emerged from dense star clusters and ultraluminous X-ray sources. They may be the missing evolutionary link between stellar-mass and supermassive black holes — the seeds from which the largest black holes grew across cosmic time. Gravitational waves are a prime tool for finding them and probing their masses and spins. Because IMBHs in merging binaries radiate across a wide frequency range (roughly mHz to Hz), they are compelling targets for multiband studies with current and future detectors.

During my PhD I contributed to the semi-analytic population-synthesis code [B-POP](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2026arXiv260320430A/abstract), and I now run N-body simulations of IMBHs in star clusters with [N-body6++GPU](https://github.com/nbodyx/Nbody6ppGPU).
